# Leave policy

Annual leave.

Every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days carry over into the next year and expire on 31 March.

Sick leave.

Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than three consecutive days.

Parental leave.

Primary caregivers get 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers get 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken any time in the first year.
