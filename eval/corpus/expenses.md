# Travel and expenses

Approval.

All business travel must be approved by your line manager before you book. Flights over 6 hours may be booked in premium economy.

Claims.

Submit receipts through the finance portal within 30 days of the expense. Claims submitted later than 60 days are not reimbursed.

Daily allowance.

The meal allowance while travelling is 45 US dollars per day. Alcohol is never reimbursed.
