# Shipping service levels

Delivery times.

Standard freight is delivered within 5 business days. Express freight is delivered within 2 business days and costs 40 percent more than standard.

Damaged goods.

Customers must report damaged goods within 48 hours of delivery, with photos. Approved claims are refunded within 14 days.

Tracking.

Every shipment gets a tracking number by email when it leaves the warehouse. Tracking updates are refreshed every 15 minutes.
